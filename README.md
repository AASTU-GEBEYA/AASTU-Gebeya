🛍️ AASTU ገበያ 

Your Campus. Your Marketplace.

AASTU ገበያ is a campus marketplace designed to bring AASTU shops, student sellers, cafés, and lounges together in one convenient place.

Instead of searching in different places, students can explore campus shops, discover products made or sold by fellow students, compare available options, and find cafés and lounges around campus.

Our goal is to make campus shopping easier, support student entrepreneurs, and help everyone discover more of what AASTU has to offer. 💙

✨ What to Expect 🏪 Campus Shops: Explore shops and discover their products. ☕ Cafés & Lounges: Find campus cafés, lounges, menus, and location information. 🎨 Student Products: Discover handmade goods, student businesses, and services. 🔎 Easy Discovery: Search, browse categories, and compare available options. 🛠️ Built With HTML5 CSS3 Vanilla JavaScript 🎓 About the Project 

This project is being developed by a team of six students as part of Internet Programming 1. It is a frontend-focused project that gives us an opportunity to apply what we learn while building something useful for our campus community.

🌱 Our Vision 

To make AASTU ገበያ a convenient digital hub where students can discover, shop, sell, and connect.

Made with 💙 by AASTU students, for the AASTU community.
